🎮 Connect Wii Remote
1
Remove the battery cover and press the red SYNC button on the back of your Wii Remote. The LEDs will start blinking.
2
Click Connect via Browser below. A browser dialog will appear — select your Wii Remote from the list.
3
Point the remote at your screen. D-pad navigates, A launches, Home goes back.
⚠️ Requires Chrome 89+ or Edge 89+ (WebHID). Firefox and Safari do not support WebHID. Pair the remote via SYNC, not OS Bluetooth settings.
WebHID is not available in this browser. Pair your Wii Remote via OS Bluetooth settings, then press any button — it will be detected automatically.
